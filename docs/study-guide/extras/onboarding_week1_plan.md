# Week 1 Guided Onboarding Plan (`onboarding_week1_plan.md`)

> **Objective:** Equip any newcomer without prior DTN, RFC, or simulator experience to build and run ADTNM, write custom autonomic rules, and critically interpret empirical evaluations within five focused half-day sessions.

| Session | Learning Focus | Core Readings (`docs/study-guide/`) | Practical Lab Exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | DTN fundamentals & managed-substrate framing | [`01 Executive Summary`](../chapters/01_executive_summary.md), [`02 Primer`](../chapters/02_primer_for_newcomers.md), [`03 Architecture`](../chapters/03_architecture_overview.md) | Install via `install.py`, pass `21` `DtnmaUnitTests`, and explain why routers act as managed substrates |
| **Day 2** | Scenario files, logs & drop accounting | [`04a Mechanisms`](../chapters/04a_mechanisms_telemetry_and_profiles.md), [`07 Scenario Guide & Tutorials`](../chapters/07_howto_and_tutorials.md), [`Worked Example 6`](../worked_examples/worked_examples.md) | Run `Tutorial 2` (`1 h` smoke) and reconcile ACK-pruned copies (`53`) vs live losses (`0`) in `DtnmaMetricsReport` |
| **Day 3** | State-based rules, safety shield & audit replay | [`04b Mechanisms`](../chapters/04b_mechanisms_srl_and_shield.md), [`Worked Examples 1–4`](../worked_examples/worked_examples.md), `Tutorial 3` | Author a custom `RULE/IF/THEN` policy with a `PRIORITY: 10` catch-all rule and verify `traceable_ari_ratio: 1.0000` |
| **Day 4** | Adapter architecture & codebase navigation | [`04c Mechanisms`](../chapters/04c_mechanisms_adapters_and_remote.md), [`05 Codebase Tour`](../chapters/05_codebase_tour.md), [`Worked Example 5`](../worked_examples/worked_examples.md) | Inspect `ManagedEpidemicRouter.java` & `DtnmaManagedRouter.java` and run `validate_mobility_scenarios.py --smoke` |
| **Day 5** | Empirical rigor & mobility coordination | [`04d Mobility & Reporting`](../chapters/04d_mechanisms_mobility_and_reporting.md), [`06 Evaluation & Results`](../chapters/06_evaluation_and_results.md), [`Appendix A`](../appendices/appendix_a_rfc_conformance.md) | Contrast `M2-Mob` and its ablations against `M2-SRL`, `M0e-25`, `M1-Remote`, `CA-SnW`, and `Managed*` substrates |
